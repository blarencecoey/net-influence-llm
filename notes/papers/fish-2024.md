# Fish et al. 2024

- LLM pricing agents in oligopoly quickly and autonomously reach supracompetitive prices and profits; also holds in auctions.
- Small "innocuous" prompt wording changes shift the degree of supracompetitive pricing.
- Behavioural analysis points to price-war avoidance as a mechanism.

## Why it matters for us
- Motivation citation: LLM collusion is **tacit** — no channel, driven by reacting to history. That is exactly H2's case, where same-round net influence should fail.
- Prompt sensitivity → fix and log exact prompts per condition; don't vary wording across conditions.

# Rose et al. 2026 (NARCBench)

Cited in the Q1.4 prompt as the interpretability alternative to [[bonjour-2022]].

## Method
- Linear "deception direction" per model: logistic regression on standardised activations from instructed-collusion runs; agent score = projection on the direction.
- Five group-level statistics over the per-agent scores:
  1. Peak Suspicion: max score (one anomalous agent).
  2. Deception Split: mean(top 2) − mean(bottom 2). **Assumes k = 2 colluders out of n = 4**, same assumption as Bonjour.
  3. Asymmetry Probe: logistic regression on variance, range, split, mean.
  4. Sorted Concatenation: sort agents by score, concat activations, PCA-50, logistic regression.
  5. Activation Alignment: mean pairwise cosine similarity of activations (representational shift).
- Models: Qwen3-32B, Llama-3.1-70B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-32B, GPT-OSS-20B.

## Benchmark
- CORE: 50 committee-vote scenarios, 4 agents, random subset told to collude via a pre-game private channel. Train split.
- TRANSFER: 6 structurally different families (code-review sabotage, pump-and-dump, CI/CD, RAG poisoning, policy slanting, news framing). Zero-shot.
- STEGO: blackjack card counting with agent-invented table-talk encodings. Zero-shot.

## Results
- 1.00 AUROC in-distribution on all models.
- TRANSFER: 0.73–0.93 AUROC on Llama-70B; at 1% FPR best recall only 0.38.
- STEGO: 0.99–1.00 AUROC; Activation Alignment gets 1.00 recall at 1% FPR.
- Emergent-collusion "Verdict" test (Qwen3): best AUROC 0.73; only 2 of 229 collusive episodes verbalised the coordination.

## Stated limitations
- GLRT interpretations assume independent normal scores; they acknowledge independence fails in general.
- Partial transfer from instructed to emergent collusion; correlational, not causal, labels.
- AUROC hides low-FPR performance; sequential detection left to future work.
- Ethics statement: publishing detection research may help evasion.

## Why it matters for us
- Same structural assumption as Bonjour (two colluders) → both lines of work share the "k = 2" gap that C4 tests.
- Needs open weights + GPUs (2–4× 24–48 GB). Not feasible on the sprint laptop budget; use as a **related-work comparison**, not a baseline we run.
- Does not cite Bonjour or use mutual information. An action-level black-box detector is a genuine complement: works on API models where activations are unavailable.
- Their Verdict result (collusion rarely verbalised) supports using actions, not transcripts, as the signal.
- Mentions Das et al. 2026 ("You Can't Escape Your Own Activations"): probe ranking largely survives monitoring-aware colluders, fixed-threshold recall drops. Secondhand; full text not readable when checked.
